TAXI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TAXI (“we”, “us”, “our”) — the operator of the TAXI mobile application (the “App”), a ride-hailing and recurring-transport service operating in Mauritania — collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information. By creating an account or using the App, you agree to this Policy.

1. Who this applies to

The App serves several types of users: riders (customers who book trips), drivers, dispatchers / support agents, the operator/owner, and subscription customers (parents or employers arranging recurring school, university or work transport). This Policy covers all of them.

2. Information we collect

Account & identity

  • Phone number — used to create your account and send a one-time verification code (by SMS/WhatsApp).
  • Name and, for drivers, profile details.

Location

  • Riders: your device’s precise location to set your pickup point, estimate fares and show nearby drivers, and to track your trip in progress.
  • Drivers: your precise location, including in the background and when the App is closed, while you are set to “Online”. This lets riders and our dispatch see your live position, match you to nearby requests, and operate trips and scheduled runs. Background collection stops when you go Offline or log out. See Section 6.

Trips, rides & scheduled runs

  • Pickup and destination points, route, distance, duration, fare, fare mode (fixed or metered), timestamps, and trip/run status and history.
  • For scheduled subscriptions: pickup order, pickup/drop-off and handover confirmations, and “not riding today” absences.

Driver information

  • Vehicle details and driver/licence information you submit when applying to drive.
  • Approval and vetting status, including whether a criminal-record check has been verified and gender (used only to assign eligible drivers to school groups and women-only groups).
  • Wallet balance, subscription daily/plan charges, and top-up records.

Subscription & children’s information (school / work transport)

  • Passenger names and phone numbers.
  • For children: name, gender, school and class/grade, pickup location, and the authorized pickup persons you designate (name, phone, relationship).
  • Schedule (service days, morning/return times), destination, and pricing/offer details.
  • This information is provided by the guardian/customer who arranges the subscription and is used only to operate that transport service. See Section 7.

Payments

  • For top-ups and subscription payments: the amount and the mobile-money transaction reference you submit (e.g. Bankily, Masrvi), and cash-receipt confirmations.
  • We do not collect or store bank-card numbers, mobile-money PINs, or banking credentials. Payments happen in your mobile-money app; we only record the reference for verification.

Notifications & device data

  • A push-notification token so we can send ride, run, payment and safety alerts.
  • Basic device/technical data provided by our infrastructure (e.g. app version, device type) needed to deliver and secure the service.

Safety

  • SOS alerts you raise during a run, with your location at that moment, and automatic alerts (e.g. an unusually long stop) sent to support.

3. How we use your information

  • Create and secure your account and verify your phone number.
  • Match riders with drivers, calculate fares, and provide live tracking and navigation.
  • Operate recurring subscriptions: group passengers, assign drivers, generate daily runs, and confirm pickups, drop-offs and handovers.
  • Send operational notifications (driver on the way, arrived, trip/run updates, invoices, reminders, safety alerts).
  • Process the driver subscription (daily fee/plans) and customer billing, and verify payments.
  • Protect users’ safety, prevent fraud and abuse, handle complaints, and provide support.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

  • Between users, as needed for the service: a driver sees the rider’s pickup (and, for scheduled runs, the passengers and authorized pickup persons needed for that run); a rider/guardian sees the assigned driver’s live location and vehicle details.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf:
    • Google Firebase (authentication, database, cloud functions, push messaging) and Google Maps / Routes (maps, routing, distance/ETA).
    • ChinqIT for delivering one-time verification codes by SMS/WhatsApp.
  • Authorities when required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of users and the public.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

5. Legal bases

We process personal data to perform our contract with you (providing the service), with your consent (for example, device location and push notifications, which you can withdraw in your device settings), for our legitimate interests (safety, fraud prevention, improving the service), and to meet legal obligations.

6. Background location (drivers)

So that riders and dispatch can see a driver’s live position and we can operate trips and scheduled runs reliably, the App collects a driver’s location in the background, even when the App is closed or not in use, but only while the driver is set to “Online.” This location data is used solely to provide the ride-hailing and transport service (live tracking, matching, navigation and run operations). It is not used for advertising. Background collection stops as soon as the driver goes Offline or logs out, and you can disable location access at any time in your device settings (doing so will prevent you from going online or receiving trips).

7. Children’s information

Our school-transport subscriptions involve information about children, which is provided and controlled by the child’s parent or guardian when they set up a subscription. We use this information only to arrange and operate that child’s transport (identifying the child for the driver, confirming safe handover to authorized persons, and notifying the guardian). Children do not create their own accounts. A guardian can view, update or delete a child’s information, or cancel the subscription, at any time, or contact us for help.

8. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service. Trip, run and financial/billing records may be retained longer where required for accounting, tax, dispute-resolution or legal reasons. When no longer needed, data is deleted or anonymized.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures (including encrypted connections and access controls on our backend) to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and limit access to those who need it to run the service.

10. Your rights & choices

  • Access & correction: view and update your profile in the App, or contact us.
  • Location & notifications: control these in your device settings at any time.
  • Account & data deletion: see Section 11.

11. How to delete your account and data

You can permanently delete your account and associated personal data directly in the App: open the menu and choose “Delete account.” This removes your profile, your saved information (and, for guardians, your children’s information), and signs you out. Financial and billing records may be retained for the period required by law (see Section 8). If you need help, contact us at oussama.mohamedelhadi@gmail.com and we will delete your account and data on request.

12. International data transfers

Our service providers (such as Google Firebase) store and process data on servers that may be located outside Mauritania (our backend is hosted in the European region). We rely on these providers’ safeguards for such transfers.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and change the “Last updated” date. Significant changes may also be notified in the App.
