TAXI — Delete your account & data

App: TAXI · Developer: TAXI

This page explains how to delete your TAXI account and the personal data associated with it.

Delete your account from inside the app

  1. Open the TAXI app and sign in.
  2. Open the menu (the drawer, top-left).
  3. Tap “Delete account.”
  4. Confirm on the dialog. Your account is permanently deleted and you are signed out.

Request deletion by email

If you can’t access the app, email us from the phone number/account you used and ask us to delete your account: oussama.mohamedelhadi@gmail.com. We will delete your account and data on request.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in (phone number, name, profile).
  • Your saved information — and, for parents/guardians, your children’s information (names, school, authorized pickup persons).
  • Your subscription requests, driver application and related personal data.
  • Your push-notification token (you stop receiving notifications).

What may be kept

  • Financial and billing records (trip fares, invoices, payment references) may be retained where required for accounting, tax or legal reasons. These are kept only for the period required by law and are then deleted or anonymized.
Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. For full details see our Privacy Policy (Section 11).
